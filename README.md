# My Favorite Horror Movie

## The Medium (2021)

**Genre:** Horror / Mystery

The Medium is my favorite horror movie. It's a Thai horror movie about spirits and possession. I like how the movie looks like a real documentary.

### Why I Like It

- The CCTV scenes were the scariest part for me.
- The camera footage made everything feel real.
- I like the creepy atmosphere.
- The movie gets more disturbing as it goes on.

### My Favorite Part

My favorite part is definitely the CCTV scene. It was really creepy because you can see what happens inside the house at night. I think that scene is what made me like this movie a lot.

### My Rating

**9/10**

*One of the few horror movies that actually scared me.*
